What this study borrows—and what it risks.

Viewed reference
Super lamp prototype, Martine Bedin for Memphis, made by Fausto Celati, 1981, V&A. The viewed image shows a rounded blue body, wheels, and bright colored lamp sockets.
Design choices
A rounded frame, offset color shapes, and a mix of serif and sans-serif type layer visual languages around the same product.
Intended effect
The playful layering may make the small color detail feel more expressive while keeping the message about an easygoing tee. This is a hypothesis.
Risk
The extra colors and layers may feel busy or too playful for the calm, sleek mood.
