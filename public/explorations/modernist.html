What this study borrows—and what it risks.

Viewed reference
Drawing, Edward McKnight Kauffer, 1934, V&A. Its high-contrast geometric figure and diagonal movement are visible in the museum image.
Design choices
A clear two-column grid, a strong type scale, and restrained labels organize the product before the supporting detail. One blue-and-coral mark carries the color accent. System fonts and simple CSS geometry keep this study straightforward to produce.
Intended effect
The quiet field may preserve a calm impression while the small accent makes the shirt feel less plain. This is a hypothesis.
Risk
The strict grid and tiny accent may feel too restrained or less sleek than the evening audience expects.
