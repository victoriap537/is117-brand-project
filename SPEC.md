## Ethical persuasion

Choose two applications that help the visitor make a voluntary, informed decision. Record their real implementation and mechanism rather than a principle name alone. Required size selection or validation alone does not establish consistency. Do not invent social proof, scarcity, authority, or product facts.

The learner selected an AI-proposed optional style-preference choice—“Keep it relaxed / Show me the graphic detail”—and chose the suggested follow-up for “Keep it relaxed.” The reversible draft implements an optional, no-default radio choice: “Keep it relaxed” reveals a note about the white shirt and small accent; “Show me the graphic detail” reveals the shirt-cropped symbol. This is a proposed consistency application: a voluntary preference is followed by matching information. The learner selected the feature and first follow-up from AI-proposed choices; assess the actual rendered behavior before treating the application as final.

The learner approved exploring an AI-proposed free styling tip, then selected the suggestion to echo the shirt's navy or yellow in one accessory. After AI-provided examples, they selected the reason that repeating one color can make the outfit feel intentional while keeping it relaxed. The reversible draft now offers this as a free, no-sign-up styling idea; after viewing it, the learner broadly approved the tip. This is a proposed reciprocity application; the selection and explanation were assisted, and no customer benefit is validated.
